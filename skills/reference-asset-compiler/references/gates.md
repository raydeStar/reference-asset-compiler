# Gate evidence

## Modeling

Attach neutral fixed front, three-quarter, side, and back renders plus any
profile-specific closeups. Review identity, anatomy, construction, continuity,
part count, and dimensions. Do not promote because a mesh merely imports.

## Texture

Attach the approved topology hash, base-color review, material-response review,
and fixed views. Reject displaced facial landmarks, stamped duplicates, seams,
specks, projection outlines, and baked lighting artifacts.

## Rig and deformation

Attach skeleton/chain audit, influence audit, clean round-trip report, and posed
closeups of hard deformation zones. Humanoids need hands, wrists, elbows,
shoulders, neck, waist, knees, and ankles.

## UE5 and cook

Attach native import statistics, exact skeleton path, in-map screenshots, motion
review, map-check report, and cook/package result. Editor-only evidence cannot
complete the cook gate.
